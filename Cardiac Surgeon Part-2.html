How to Become a Cardiac Surgeon

Step 1: School Education

You must take science in 11th and 12th (Physics, Chemistry, Biology).

Strong basics are very important.

Step 2: Entrance Exam

You need to clear medical entrance exams like NEET.

This allows you to enter a medical college.

Step 3: MBBS Degree

Complete MBBS (5.5 years including internship).

This is the basic medical degree.

Step 4: Postgraduate Study

Do MS in General Surgery (3 years).

Step 5: Super Specialization

Study MCh in Cardiac Surgery (3 years).

This is where you become a heart surgeon.

Final Stage

After years of training, you become a qualified cardiac surgeon.

You can now perform heart surgeries and save lives.
