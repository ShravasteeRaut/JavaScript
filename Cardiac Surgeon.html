Life of a Cardiac Surgeon

A cardiac surgeon undergoes years of intense education and training.

Their daily life includes long hours in operation theatres performing complex heart surgeries.

They work under high pressure but save lives, making the career deeply rewarding.

Why It’s Inspiring
